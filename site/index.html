FAQ

Questions, answered first

Is this a leadership course?

No. It’s English for people who already lead: updates, meetings, feedback and hard news — in English, with clarity.

What level of English do I need?

B2 to C1 on the CEFR scale. Not sure? Take the 1-minute self-check.

Is it live? Are sessions recorded?

It’s 100% live by video call, and sessions are not recorded by default. There are no self-paced lessons.

How much time does it take?

Two 60-minute sessions a week for 12 weeks — 22 sessions. No homework.

What if I miss a session?

Week 6 includes a make-up session. To get the certificate, you need to attend at least 17 of the 22 sessions.

How do I pay?

By Zelle, after you register: $500 USD once, or 3 installments of $175 ($525 in total). Within 24 hours we email you the instructions.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations and refunds are explained in our Terms of Use.

What do I get at the end?

A certificate with a performance profile across six criteria, when you meet the attendance requirement and complete the final simulation.
